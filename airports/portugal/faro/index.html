Before you land

One terminal, and the busiest Saturdays in Portugal

Peak summer Saturdays at Faro are the busiest arrivals in the country. The terminal is single and simple, but the hall gets full. Your driver waits with your name at the arrivals exit.

The A22 is toll-free since January 2025

The Via do Infante carries no tolls, which makes the whole Algarve quicker and cheaper to reach than it was. Any remaining road charge is inside your fixed price.

Portuguese TVDE and taxi licensing

Every driver holds a TVDE or taxi licence issued by IMT. We check the licence and the insurance before a company takes its first ride.

Most passengers are not going to Faro

Almost everything is west on the A22 — Albufeira, Vilamoura, Lagos. Resorts spread over several kilometres, so give the hotel name and not just the town. It is the single most common cause of a late arrival.

The airport

Faro Airport serves the whole Algarve, and most passengers are not going to Faro itself. Everything west — Albufeira, Vilamoura, Lagos — is on the A22 motorway. Arrivals is a single hall and the meeting point is straight through customs.

  • Peak summer Saturdays are the busiest arrivals in Portugal. Allow extra time and expect the terminal to be full.
  • The A22 has electronic tolls only. Our price includes them, which is not always the case with local taxis — worth checking if you compare.
