The airport

Porto Airport is 11 km north of the city in Maia. It is a small, easy airport — arrivals to car is usually under ten minutes. The meeting point is the arrivals hall, straight ahead as you leave baggage reclaim.

  • Porto has almost no queue at passport control compared to Lisbon. Twenty minutes from landing to car is normal.
  • The metro goes to the centre but takes 35 minutes with luggage and a change. With two or more people a transfer usually costs less than the fares.

Where people go

Prices are for one to four passengers. Larger groups travel in a van, which costs more but still one fixed price for the whole group.
