The airport

Lisbon Airport sits inside the city, seven kilometres from the centre, which is unusual for a European capital. Arrivals is on the ground floor of Terminal 1; Terminal 2 handles low-cost departures only, so you will almost certainly land at T1. The meeting point is the arrivals hall after customs — your driver waits there with your name.

  • Passport control can take 40 minutes in summer at peak arrival times. Your driver tracks the flight and waits.
  • Terminal 2 has no arrivals. If you booked a pick-up there, it is wrong — tell us and we move it.
