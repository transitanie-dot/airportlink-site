The journey

Sintra is close in kilometres but slow in practice: the road up to the historic centre is narrow and winds. Cars cannot reach Pena Palace itself. Ask to be dropped at your hotel and use the local shuttle for the palaces.

Arriving at Lisbon Airport

Lisbon Airport sits inside the city, seven kilometres from the centre, which is unusual for a European capital. Arrivals is on the ground floor of Terminal 1; Terminal 2 handles low-cost departures only, so you will almost certainly land at T1. The meeting point is the arrivals hall after customs — your driver waits there with your name.

  • Passport control can take 40 minutes in summer at peak arrival times. Your driver tracks the flight and waits.
  • Terminal 2 has no arrivals. If you booked a pick-up there, it is wrong — tell us and we move it.

What is included

  • A private vehicle for your group. No sharing, no other stops.
  • A fixed price with tolls and taxes in. Nothing is added at the end.
  • Flight tracking. Land late and the pick-up moves, not the price.
  • 60 minutes of free waiting after the flight lands.
  • Free cancellation until 24 hours before pick-up.
  • One suitcase and one piece of hand luggage per passenger. More than that, tell us when you book.

Questions

How long does it take to get from Lisbon Airport to Sintra?

About 40 minutes for the 30 km, outside peak traffic. Your driver knows the route and follows the flight, so a delayed landing moves the pick-up rather than costing you the transfer.

How much is a transfer from Lisbon Airport to Sintra?

From €88 for up to four passengers, and €150 for a group of five to eight in a van. The price is fixed when you book — tolls and taxes included, nothing added at the end.

What happens if my flight is late?

We track the flight number you give us. The driver waits and adjusts to the actual landing time, with 60 minutes of free waiting after the plane touches down.

Where do I meet the driver at Lisbon Airport?

In the arrivals hall after you clear customs. The day before your trip we send you the driver's name, phone number and vehicle, and they contact you with the exact spot.

Can I cancel?

Free up to 24 hours before pick-up, refunded in full and automatically. On many routes you can also book without paying and we only charge the card 48 hours before you travel.
