The journey

Half an hour from the airport. Vilamoura is built around the marina and the golf courses, and the resorts spread out for a few kilometres — the marina itself is the reference point most drivers use.

Arriving at Faro Airport

Faro Airport serves the whole Algarve, and most passengers are not going to Faro itself. Everything west — Albufeira, Vilamoura, Lagos — is on the A22 motorway. Arrivals is a single hall and the meeting point is straight through customs.

  • Peak summer Saturdays are the busiest arrivals in Portugal. Allow extra time and expect the terminal to be full.
  • The A22 has electronic tolls only. Our price includes them, which is not always the case with local taxis — worth checking if you compare.

What is included

  • A private vehicle for your group. No sharing, no other stops.
  • A fixed price with tolls and taxes in. Nothing is added at the end.
  • Flight tracking. Land late and the pick-up moves, not the price.
  • 60 minutes of free waiting after the flight lands.
  • Free cancellation until 24 hours before pick-up.
  • One suitcase and one piece of hand luggage per passenger. More than that, tell us when you book.

Questions

How long does it take to get from Faro Airport to Vilamoura?

About 30 minutes for the 32 km, outside peak traffic. Your driver knows the route and follows the flight, so a delayed landing moves the pick-up rather than costing you the transfer.

How much is a transfer from Faro Airport to Vilamoura?

From €91 for up to four passengers, and €155 for a group of five to eight in a van. The price is fixed when you book — tolls and taxes included, nothing added at the end.

What happens if my flight is late?

We track the flight number you give us. The driver waits and adjusts to the actual landing time, with 60 minutes of free waiting after the plane touches down.

Where do I meet the driver at Faro Airport?

In the arrivals hall after you clear customs. The day before your trip we send you the driver's name, phone number and vehicle, and they contact you with the exact spot.

Can I cancel?

Free up to 24 hours before pick-up, refunded in full and automatically. On many routes you can also book without paying and we only charge the card 48 hours before you travel.
