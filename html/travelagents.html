Partner questions

The things agencies ask us first.

01

Is there a fee or a minimum number of bookings?

No. There is no subscription, no joining fee and no minimum volume. If you book once and never again, nothing happens.

02

Who does the driver contact, me or my traveller?

Your traveller. You enter their name and phone when booking, and that is what reaches the driver. You stay on the booking as the agency and receive the paperwork, so you are not relaying messages at dawn.

03

Do you contact my clients about anything else?

Never. We use the passenger's details for that transfer only, because the driver needs to reach them on the day. No marketing, no newsletters, no upsells.

04

Can I include the transfer in a package or itinerary?

Of course. How you present it to your traveller is your commercial relationship, not ours. We handle the transfer and stay out of the way.

05

How do I pay?

By card, per booking, at the moment you book. The monthly statement is a summary for your accounts, not an invoice to settle.

06

What if my traveller cancels?

Cancel from your dashboard up to 12 hours before pick-up and the full amount goes back to your card automatically. Inside 12 hours the transfer is already assigned to a driver, so the booking stands.

07

How long does approval take?

Normally one working day. A website or social page makes it quicker, because it saves us asking.
