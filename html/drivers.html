Partner questions

What companies ask us first.

01

Do you take a commission?

No. The fee shown on the offer is transferred to you in full. Our margin sits in what the passenger pays, which is a separate number you never have to think about.

02

Am I obliged to accept rides?

Never. Pass on anything that does not fit and nothing happens. Your schedule stays yours.

03

When do I get paid?

Once a month. At the start of each month you receive a statement covering every ride completed in the previous one, and payment goes to the account on file.

04

Can individual drivers apply?

No. We partner with licensed, insured companies. If you drive your own company’s vehicle, register the company and add yourself as one of its drivers.

05

Which countries do you operate in?

We are opening market by market. Create an account wherever you are — if we are not live in your country yet, you go on the list and get first pick of the work when we arrive.

06

What happens when a document expires?

Offers pause automatically on the expiry date, and we remind you 30 days before. Upload the new one and you are back in immediately.

07

How long does verification take?

We review complete submissions by hand, usually within a few working days. If something is wrong we tell you which document and why.
