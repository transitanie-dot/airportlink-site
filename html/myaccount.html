Frequently asked questions

What does “card saved” mean?

You booked without paying. We hold your card securely with Stripe and charge it 48 hours before pick-up. Cancel before then and nothing is ever charged.

What if the payment fails?

We try again a few times and let you know. If it keeps failing the booking is cancelled, so a driver is not sent to a trip nobody paid for.

How do I cancel a booking?

Go to "My trips" and press Cancel on the booking. It is free until 24 hours before pick-up and the refund is automatic. After that, contact support.

When do I get my money back?

The refund is issued immediately on our side. Your bank usually takes 5 to 10 working days to show it.

What if my flight is delayed?

If you gave us your flight number we track the arrival and adjust the pick-up. Otherwise, message support as soon as you know.

Can I change the pick-up address?

Contact support with your booking reference and we will update it if the driver has not started.
