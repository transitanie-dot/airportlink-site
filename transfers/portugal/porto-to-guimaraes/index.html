The journey

Guimarães is under an hour on the A7. The medieval centre is pedestrian, so the drop-off is at the edge — usually Largo do Toural or your hotel if it has access.

Arriving at Porto Airport

Porto Airport is 11 km north of the city in Maia. It is a small, easy airport — arrivals to car is usually under ten minutes. The meeting point is the arrivals hall, straight ahead as you leave baggage reclaim.

  • Porto has almost no queue at passport control compared to Lisbon. Twenty minutes from landing to car is normal.
  • The metro goes to the centre but takes 35 minutes with luggage and a change. With two or more people a transfer usually costs less than the fares.

Before you land

One terminal, and a fast one

Porto is small and efficient. Twenty minutes from landing to car is normal, against forty in Lisbon. Your driver meets you in the arrivals hall, straight ahead from baggage reclaim.

No tolls on the way into Porto

The VCI into the city is toll-free. Going to Braga or the Douro means motorway tolls, and those are inside your fixed price.

Portuguese TVDE and taxi licensing

Every driver holds a TVDE or taxi licence issued by IMT. Licence and insurance are checked before a company takes its first ride.

The Ribeira is steeper than it looks

If your hotel is down by the river, the last streets are narrow and often pedestrian. The driver gets as close as the car allows, usually a two-minute walk.

Questions

How long does it take to get from Porto Airport to Guimarães?

About 55 minutes for the 70 km, outside peak traffic. Your driver knows the route and follows the flight, so a delayed landing moves the pick-up rather than costing you the transfer.

How much is a transfer from Porto Airport to Guimarães?

From €152 for up to four passengers, and €258 for a group of five to eight in a van. The price is fixed when you book — tolls and taxes included, nothing added at the end.

What happens if my flight is late?

We track the flight number you give us. The driver waits and adjusts to the actual landing time, with 60 minutes of free waiting after the plane touches down.

Where do I meet the driver at Porto Airport?

In the arrivals hall after you clear customs. The day before your trip we send you the driver's name, phone number and vehicle, and they contact you with the exact spot.

Can I cancel?

Free up to 24 hours before pick-up, refunded in full and automatically. On many routes you can also book without paying and we only charge the card 48 hours before you travel.
